Imagine Studio — editing Grok Imagine clips
Why

The round trip is the slowest part of making a Grok film.

Every new shot means another download, another import into a desktop editor, another trip back to Grok to generate the next take. Imagine Studio removes the trip.

Without it

  1. Download every clip, one by one
  2. Rename them so you know which is which
  3. Open a desktop editor, import, cut
  4. Go back to Grok for the next shot
  5. Download again… repeat

With Imagine Studio

  1. Click + Bin on the clips you like (or auto-collect)
  2. Trim, reorder, add transitions and music in the docked panel
  3. Send the next prompt to Grok from the same panel
  4. New takes land in the right shot automatically
  5. Export MP4 — done
Features

Everything between “generate” and “publish”.

Collect & pick

Every Grok video, in one bin

The studio sees the videos Grok generates for you — with their prompts — and collects them in one click, or automatically.

  • Hover to scrub through any clip
  • Pick, reject and rate with the keyboard in Review mode
  • One-click delete with Undo, and a clean-up for rejects
  • “Last frame → Grok” to continue a shot seamlessly
Edit

A real timeline — iMovie-level, inside your Grok tab

Trim, split and reorder on a magnetic track, then make it look finished.

  • 22 transitions, titles in 11 styles, Ken Burns, colour looks
  • Picture in picture, cutaway, split screen, green screen
  • Reverse, freeze frame, instant replay, crop & rotate
  • Music or voice from a file or URL, EQ, ducking, voice-over recording
Dialogue planner

Long dialogue? One part per 15-second video.

Paste the whole scene. It's split into parts that each fit one generated video — never cutting a line in the middle — and every part becomes a shot.

  • Each generated video is tagged with its part (P1, P2…) — you always know which video says which lines
  • Scene, speaker voices and phrases go into every prompt
  • Assemble in dialogue order and add subtitles in one click
Prompt workshop

The phrases you type every time, one click away

Keep “nobody speaks”, “camera angle from top”, “no text on screen” as chips under every prompt. Click in, click out.

  • 120 prepared phrases in 10 categories, plus your own
  • Phrase sets apply several at once
  • Shot list, prompt builder, character bible, reference images
  • Send to Grok's prompt box — takes link back to their shot
Export

MP4 in seconds. In your browser.

Rendered frame by frame with WebCodecs on your own machine — the preview and the file match exactly.

  • MP4 (H.264 + AAC) or WebM, any resolution and frame rate
  • PNG stills and WAV audio mixdowns
  • No upload, no account, no watermark

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Free and open source. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and Opera (Chrome 116+).

1Download

the extension zip and unzip it.

2Open extensions

Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

3Load it

Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

4Open Grok

Go to grok.com/imagine and click the STUDIO tab — or press Alt+Shift+S.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes — free and open source (MIT). No account, no subscription, no watermark.

Is it made by xAI?

No. Imagine Studio is an independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by xAI. “Grok” is a trademark of its owner. It works with your own Grok account in your own browser.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere. Clips, projects and exports stay in your browser's local storage. The extension has no server and no analytics. Privacy policy.

Does it need a Grok subscription?

It edits whatever Grok Imagine generates for you. If you can generate videos on grok.com, you can edit them with Imagine Studio.

Can I add my own music or voice?

Yes — from a file, any URL, another clip's sound, or record a voice-over straight onto the timeline. Ducking lowers the music under the voice automatically.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and Opera on desktop (Chrome 116 or later).

I found a bug / I want a feature

Open an issue on GitHub — it's read.
